Put your own background pictures in this folder with exactly these names:

  hero-bg.jpg   -> big photo at the top of the home page
  page-bg.jpg   -> header band on Jobs, Dashboard, About pages
  auth-bg.jpg   -> background of the Login and Sign up pages
  cta-bg.jpg    -> "Why CareerConnect" photo and the purple "Hiring?" band

If a file is missing, the website automatically uses a free online photo instead.
Tip: use landscape pictures about 1920 x 1080 pixels and under 500 KB so the page loads fast.
